

1. Start the program.
2. Initialize the parking slots and create the vehicle records.
3. Display the parking management menu.
4. Accept the vehicle details from the user.
5. Check for available parking slots.
6. Allocate an available slot to the vehicle.
7. Store the vehicle entry details.
8. When a vehicle exits, retrieve its parking record.
9. Calculate the parking duration and total parking charges.
10. Release the occupied parking slot.
11. Update the parking availability and save the records to a file.
12. Repeat the process until the user selects Exit.
13. Stop the program.
